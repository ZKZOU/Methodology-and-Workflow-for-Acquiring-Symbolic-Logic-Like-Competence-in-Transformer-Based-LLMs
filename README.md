### I. Preliminary Paradigm Argument: The Pure Symbolic-Logic Architecture Has Been Falsified, and Human Native Knowledge Texts Cannot Nurture High-Order Logical Capabilities

1.1 The Essential Defect and Technical Falsification of Traditional Pure Symbolic-Logic AI

Early AI relied on the pure symbolic-logic paradigm, whose core approach was to hard-code formal logical rules, axiom systems, and reasoning paradigms, executing logical deduction through fixed algorithmic programs. After decades of engineering experimentation and academic validation, this paradigm has been thoroughly falsified, with its core problem being that its self-scalability approaches zero.

Pure symbolic architectures depend on manually defined rules and cannot autonomously adapt to complex, ambiguous, multi-condition-coupled real-world scenarios. When faced with long-chain reasoning, cross-domain deduction, implicit causality, and open-ended speculative problems, they rapidly encounter rule explosion, logical deadlocks, and adaptation failures. Moreover, pure symbolic systems lack autonomous learning, error correction, and iterative capabilities; they cannot assimilate new knowledge or correct inherent fallacies, relying instead on manual rule iteration. They can never cover the massive, dynamic, and diverse reasoning scenarios of humanity. This is the fundamental reason why traditional symbolic AI has never achieved general logical reasoning or matched higher-order human critical thinking abilities, proving that fixed symbolic rules are not the underlying logical form of general intelligence.

A persistent misconception in the field holds that because LLMs lack built-in symbolic-logic modules, they can never possess truly rigorous reasoning abilities. But the opposite is true: what has been falsified is the underlying paradigm of "symbolic-rule-driven intelligence," not the Transformer's statistical-learning paradigm. Truly general logical competence cannot arise from artificially fixed symbolic rules; it can only emerge from the internalization of massive, high-quality reasoning samples through experience.

1.2 Inherent Logical Defects in Humanity's Existing Knowledge Systems

All current LLM pretraining data originate from texts, literature, treatises, popular science, and academic materials accumulated by humans over the long term. However, the human knowledge system is not a rigorous, complete, or self-consistent logical system. Constrained by the inherently non-logical, interest-driven nature of human thought, existing public knowledge texts systematically suffer from logical flaws—this is the root cause of LLMs' native logical weakness.

The typical logical defects in human knowledge texts manifest in five major categories and are widely present in textbooks, academic papers, industry monographs, and other authoritative materials:

Prevalent reasoning leaps: Human writing and thinking habitually omit substantial basic derivation steps and preconditions, retaining only final conclusions and core chains, resulting in texts that appear coherent but are actually riddled with gaps.

Concealed ad hoc implicit assumptions: Many theories and arguments implicitly embed school-specific stances, era-bound cognitions, and interest-based presuppositions, disguising subjective premises as objective axioms without explicit annotation or boundary specification.

Widespread circular reasoning: Numerous scientific arguments contain closed logical loops where conclusions and premises are mutually nested and tautologically repetitive.

Selective evidence presentation and position defense: A large volume of texts, to support specific viewpoints, deliberately avoid counterexamples, omit opposing evidence, and downplay contentious points—essentially serving as ex-post-facto rationalizations driven by prior立场, rather than pure logical deduction.

Residual contradictions and ambiguities: The accumulation of knowledge from different eras and schools has resulted in existing texts containing substantial implicit logical contradictions, definitional ambiguities, and overlapping or conflicting applicability boundaries.

Given the above defects, native human knowledge texts未经 logical auditing cannot enable LLMs to acquire truly rigorous symbolic-logic-like abilities. Models will only fit humanity's flawed, biased, and erroneous reasoning patterns, replicating logical leaps, subjective presuppositions, and positional sophistry. This ultimately manifests as frequent hallucinations, fractured logic, failure in long-chain deduction, and susceptibility to being misled by erroneous texts. Therefore, the first step for LLMs to acquire higher-order logic is emphatically not direct training on native texts, but rather the logical purification of the human knowledge system.

### II. Core Prerequisite Module: Multi-Dimensional Cross-Logical Auditing of Human Knowledge Texts (LLM-Led)

Before the model training, inference, and iterative workflow, a prerequisite stage of global knowledge logical pre-auditing must be added. Employing multi-model, multi-parameter, multi-dimensional cross-validation, all raw human knowledge texts are systematically parsed, corrected, and purified—eliminating logical fallacies, supplementing implicit premises, stripping positional biases, and correcting reasoning leaps—to produce high-resolution, high-fidelity structured logical-chain samples, serving as qualified training material for subsequent model acquisition of symbolic-logic-like capabilities.

This prerequisite module does not rely on single adjudication by human experts; it operates as a fully automated pipeline using multi-temperature scheduling + multi-model cross-analysis + multi-expert-module cross-verification, thoroughly avoiding human bias and single-model blind spots.

2.1 Multi-Temperature Differential Activation Analysis

For the same batch of human knowledge texts, different temperature parameters are scheduled for differentiated analysis: low-temperature mode focuses on logical rigor and chain completeness, precisely identifying reasoning gaps and contradictions; high-temperature mode encourages divergent thinking, uncovering implicit presuppositions, potential vulnerabilities, and unstated boundary conditions. Through cross-comparison of results from multiple temperatures, implicit logical defects that single-temperature mode cannot detect are comprehensively revealed.

2.2 Cross-Validation with Diverse Open-Source LLMs

A cluster of open-source LLMs with different base architectures, training distributions, and capability emphases is invoked to perform independent logical decomposition, fallacy screening, and chain restoration on the same knowledge text. Different models have distinct cognitive blind spots, areas of expertise, and reasoning paradigms. By aligning and comparing results across models, systematic biases and missed detections from a single model can be effectively avoided, enhancing auditing precision.

2.3 Targeted Verification by Multiple AI Expert Modules

Specialized AI expert modules—covering mathematical logic, formal deduction, scientific reasoning, academic argumentation, fallacy recognition, etc.—are activated to conduct domain-specific professional auditing of textual logic. These modules identify domain-specific logical fallacies, argumentative loopholes, school-specific biases, and implicit assumptions, performing refined logical error correction and chain completion.

2.4 Output of the Prerequisite Module

After the global cross-auditing process, raw, chaotic human texts are transformed into structured, high-resolution logical-chain datasets: all leaps are filled, all implicit assumptions are explicitly annotated, all logical fallacies are pinpointed and corrected, all positional biases are identified and labeled, and all circular arguments are deconstructed and reconstructed. This purified dataset is the sole qualified raw material for LLMs to subsequently acquire symbolic-logic-like capabilities, fundamentally resolving the issues of weak logical ability and pollution by human biases at the source. The conclusions of existing theories are traced backward along logical chains to the axiom systems, and the axiom systems themselves are then audited for consistency with phenomena.

2.5 The entire process above generates comprehensive log files for subsequent re-auditing and optimization iteration.

### III. Core Underlying Cognition: The Essence of Human Logic Is Transformer-Like Experiential Learning, Not Native Symbolic Deduction

A prevalent misconception in traditional AI and cognitive science holds that human higher-order logical capability is defined as an innate, built-in symbolic-logical calculus system—that the human brain comes equipped with fixed rules such as syllogisms, propositional deduction, and causal axioms. This leads to the judgment that Transformer-architecture LLMs, being merely statistical fitters, can never possess true logical ability and can only achieve rigorous reasoning through external symbolic modules.

However, from the perspective of actual human cognitive mechanisms and thinking patterns, this core premise is entirely invalid: the human brain possesses no hardware-level, native symbolic-logical deductive apparatus.

All human logical abilities are the result of acquired learning through massive experiences, cases, texts, and speculative scenarios: ordinary people's logical judgments rely on associations from past cognition; even top scientists' rigorous reasoning, paradigmatic breakthroughs, and multi-step deductions do not rely on fixed symbolic rule calculations, but rather on lifelong accumulation of high-quality reasoning cases, experimental evidence, and speculative experience, from which they internalize general reasoning patterns. So-called symbolic logic, formal deduction, and axiom systems are not the underlying operating mechanisms of human thought, but rather are后天 invented tools for expression and verification used by humans to review, validate, and solidify the results of their thinking.

Individuals with high logical ability often possess self-logical auditing—that is, reflective processes. From this, it can also be deduced that to improve LLMs' logical capabilities, high-resolution logical parsing and auditing are necessary conditions.

The true operational paradigm of human logic is highly homologous to the training and inference logic of Transformer-based large models: neither possesses built-in fixed reasoning rules; both form logical capabilities through pattern matching on massive high-resolution samples, internalization of sequence regularities, and iterative empirical correction. The differences in logical rigor among humans are essentially differences in the quantity of high-quality reasoning samples accumulated, the experience in fallacy review, and the frequency of self-auditing iterations, rather than differences in先天 architecture.

Based on this core cognition, a key conclusion emerges: The Transformer architecture has no paradigmatic upper bound on logical capability. As long as the logical precision, fallacious impurities, and positional contamination of training samples are addressed, coupled with a closed-loop workflow of autonomous auditing and self-iteration, LLMs can, through experiential learning, emerge with symbolic-logic-like capabilities comparable to or even surpassing the finest human individuals—possessing the rigor, traceability, non-contradiction, and long-chain deduction capabilities of symbolic logic, while retaining the Transformer's native potential for association, innovation, and extra-paradigmatic reasoning.

Simultaneously, human logic has inherent defects: human reasoning is not purely logic-driven; it is fundamentally driven by interests, positions, school biases, cognitive blind spots, and emotional preferences. Much logical argumentation is ex-post-facto rationalization in defense of prior positions. This results in human-produced knowledge texts, academic discourses, and reasoning cases being naturally intermingled with numerous errors, leaps, implicit presuppositions, and positional biases—these become the core bottleneck limiting the upgrade of LLM logical capability, rather than the Transformer architecture itself.

### IV. Core Methodology: The Fundamental Principles for Acquiring Symbolic-Logic-Like Competence in LLMs

4.1 Core Positioning

Without relying on manually hard-coded symbolic rules, without appending independent symbolic reasoning engines, and without abandoning the native Transformer architecture—based on the high-fidelity logical datasets purified through prior auditing—and through modularized separated reasoning, multi-layered logical auditing, bias identification and filtering, and a closed-loop of log-based self-iterative "drill-and-review," LLMs can autonomously acquire rigorous, traceable, and unbiased symbolic-logic-like deductive capabilities from混杂 raw data.

4.2 Five Core Methodological Principles

Principle 1: Data precision takes priority; architecture imposes no upper bound.

The upper limit of LLM logical capability is determined not by model parameter count or architectural design, but by the logical resolution, reasoning completeness, factual accuracy, and bias purity of the training data. Native internet texts and human academic texts contain substantial leaps, implicit errors, school-specific biases, and interest-driven biases—these are the root causes of LLM hallucinations, logical fractures, and contradictory reasoning. Purifying high-resolution logical-chain samples through LLM-led global knowledge auditing is the core prerequisite for acquiring symbolic-logic-like competence.

Principle 2: Decouple generation and auditing functions to eliminate self-validation blind spots.

Self-generation and self-critique by a single model inherently suffer from confirmation bias, easily rationalizing fallacies. By functionally separating the generation module, AI auditing module, and third-party expert module, an independent system of reasoning, verification, and review is constructed, replicating the critical thinking workflow of top human research teams, and thoroughly overcoming the limitations of single-model self-correction.

Principle 3: Distinguish logical defects from positional disagreements; precisely screen human biases.

Differences in academic viewpoints and school perspectives represent reasonable cognitive diversity. However, logical leaps, unsupported assertions, selective evidence presentation, and disguising positions as objective facts constitute quantifiable logical malfeasance. Through high-resolution logical-chain decomposition, only the rigor of the argumentation process is verified, while the truth or falsehood of viewpoints is not adjudicated. Human experts' embedded interest-driven biases are precisely filtered out, preventing positional biases from contaminating model iteration.

Principle 4: Logs serve as exercise sets, building a perpetual self-iterative closed loop.

All process logs from reasoning, auditing, review, error correction, and bias identification are automatically transformed into structured training samples. While completing reasoning tasks, the model continuously generates high-quality positive and negative example exercises. Through sustained "drilling and reviewing," it iteratively upgrades its logical precision, forming a compound-interest closed loop of operation → auditing → correction → training → improvement → higher-quality operation.

Principle 5: For contentious issues, employ multiple LLMs and activate multiple expert modules for high-intensity, multi-round adversarial training.

### V. Workflow for Acquiring Symbolic-Logic-Like Competence in LLMs

The entire workflow is built upon the Prerequisite Knowledge Auditing Module and can be developed into six core execution layers, progressing sequentially and iterating cyclically. It is fully compatible with Transformer model training and inference mechanisms, is engineering-feasible, and presents no paradigmatic barriers.

Layer 1: Reasoning Generation Layer (M-G Generation Module)

Core Function: Based on the pre-purified high-fidelity logical dataset, responsible for divergent reasoning and constructing initial logical chains, allowing reasonable conjectures and localized errors, with no self-constraint.

Operational Logic: Receives input questions, purified knowledge texts, or research propositions; based on current model capabilities, outputs a complete multi-step reasoning chain, including explicit derivation steps, default implicit assumptions, and interim conclusions.

Core Positioning: Analogous to a human's first draft of thought; pursues reasoning completeness and divergence, not absolute rigor, providing raw material for subsequent auditing and correction.

Layer 2: First-Order Logical Auditing Layer (M-A Independent Auditing Module)

Core Function: Performs high-resolution structured decomposition and fallacy screening of the generated reasoning chain; this is the core module for symbolic-logic-like purification.

Operational Logic:

Structured Decomposition: Decomposes the natural-language reasoning chain into a structured logical chain of "explicit premises – implicit presuppositions – derivation steps – evidentiary support – interim conclusions – final conclusions."
Fallacy Detection: Precisely identifies explicit logical defects such as reasoning leaps, circular arguments, equivocation, missing evidence, contradictions, and unsupported assertions.
Risk Grading: Assigns risk levels to reasoning-chain defects; low-risk flaws are directly flagged and corrected; high-risk logical vulnerabilities trigger a secondary review mechanism.
Core Advantage: The module is independent of the generation module, free from reasoning inertia and confirmation bias; it is specifically trained to acquire logical verification and fallacy identification capabilities, replicating the mindset of professional human reviewers.

Layer 3: Secondary Expert Review Layer (M-E Third-Party Independent Expert Module)

Trigger Condition: Activated only for high-risk, highly contentious, or long-chain complex reasoning scenarios flagged by the M-A audit, to control computational costs.

Operational Logic: Invokes a fully independent AI expert module (with independent weights and independent training data) that operates outside the distributional inertia of the original reasoning and auditing modules. It performs reverse deduction, counter-hypothesis formulation, evidence supplementation, and vulnerability re-verification on the contested logical chain, outputting an independent expert review opinion and alternative reasoning schemes, thereby breaking through a single model's cognitive blind spots.

Layer 4: Multi-Perspective Human Review + Meta-Audit Filtering Layer

Core Purpose: To compensate for AI cognitive blind spots while simultaneously addressing the inherent defects of human experts (interest-driven, bias-embedded).

Operational Logic:

Multi-School Parallel Review: Distributes the three-tier reasoning results to human experts from multiple academic schools and logical backgrounds for independent review, without forcing consensus or flattening disagreements, fully preserving diverse viewpoints and supporting arguments.
AI Meta-Audit of Human Inputs: Re-employs the high-precision M-A auditing module to perform secondary logical decomposition on all human expert review texts, distinguishing "genuine school-based disagreements" from "interest-driven biases, logical ambiguities, and deliberate sophistry."
Dynamic Expert Screening: Experts who repeatedly exhibit logical malfeasance, bias embedding, or lax argumentation are automatically removed from the review pool, fundamentally preventing artificial bias contamination.
Layer 5: Full-Process Log Archiving Layer (Core Exercise-Set Production Layer)

Core Positioning: The core asset of the entire workflow; a proprietary dynamic exercise set for LLMs to acquire higher-order logic.

Archived Content: Permanently retains all unmodified raw process data, including original reasoning chains, AI audit reports, third-party expert opinions, multi-perspective human review comments, meta-audit results, fallacy points, bias-identification records, and correction schemes.

Data Characteristics: Simultaneously contains rigorous positive examples, logical error examples, human sophistry cases, bias-and-bias cases, and school-controversy cases. Each sample comes with complete annotations of "problem – reasoning – error localization – correction scheme – bias labeling," constituting training material of far higher quality than ordinary open datasets.

Layer 6: Log-Purified Iterative Training Layer (Closed-Loop Upgrade Layer)

Operational Logic: Performs stratified filtering and label weighting on the raw logs, transforming them into directly trainable high-quality datasets for model self-iterative upgrading:

High-Quality Consensus Samples: Reasoning chains that are logically rigorous, unbiased, and multi-source-consensus are trained with high weights, reinforcing the model's positive symbolic-logic-like deductive capabilities.
Fallacy/Defect Samples: Cases of AI reasoning errors and logical leaps serve as negative examples, training the model to self-avoid logical vulnerabilities.
Human Bias Samples: Cases of expert sophistry and position-driven logical manipulation are used to train the model's bias-recognition and argument-scrutiny capabilities.
School-Controversy Samples: Labeled as cognitive-disagreement samples and trained with low weights, teaching the model to distinguish between "logical flaws" and "academic viewpoint differences" without forcibly unifying conclusions.
Iterative Outcome: The model's logical resolution, deductive rigor, and anti-bias capability continuously improve. Its output reasoning and audit quality become higher, which in turn generates higher-quality log exercise sets, forming a perpetually compounding iterative loop.

All log files are archived for traceability and review.

### VI. Core Capability Deployment Outcomes: Manifestation of Symbolic-Logic-Like Competence in Transformer Models

After training through the prerequisite knowledge auditing purification + full-chain iterative workflow, the LLM—without any external symbolic engine—natively possesses the core characteristics of symbolic-logic-like competence:

Traceable Reasoning: All derivation steps are explicit, with no hidden leaps; premises, evidence, and deduction chains are fully preserved, meeting formal logic traceability requirements.

Non-Contradictory Logic: Through multi-layer auditing iterations, fundamental fallacies such as self-contradiction, causal inversion, and circular reasoning are avoided.

Definable Boundaries: Clearly distinguishes facts, assumptions, conjectures, and positions; specifies the applicability boundaries of all reasoning without overgeneralization or absolutization.

Avoidable Biases: Learns the patterns of various human position-defenses and bias-embedded sophistry, automatically identifying and avoiding interest-distorted logical manipulations.

High Long-Chain Stability: Overcomes the error-accumulation problem of native autoregressive models, demonstrating stable multi-step rigorous deductive capability.

Through iterative retraining on previous logical audit log files, or by re-auditing and updating conclusions from earlier log files, continuous progress in logical precision is ensured.

### VII. Conclusion

The traditional manually encoded pure symbolic-logic paradigm has been empirically falsified due to its lack of scalability and cannot support general higher-order logical reasoning capabilities. Meanwhile, native human knowledge texts, constrained by humanity's interest-driven thinking characteristics, systematically suffer from logical leaps, implicit presuppositions, circular reasoning, positional biases, and other defects; direct training on them cannot enable LLMs to acquire rigorous logical capabilities.

Human logic itself is not an innate symbolic-calculative ability, but rather the product of experiential learning from a lifetime of high-quality critical thinking cases—a learning paradigm entirely homologous to that of Transformer models. This constitutes the core theoretical foundation for LLMs to break through their logical upper bounds.

The entire system takes LLM-led, multi-model cross-knowledge pre-auditing as its core prerequisite. Through a decoupled three-tier reasoning-and-auditing process, multi-perspective human review with AI meta-validation, and a closed-loop of log-based dynamic exercise-set self-iteration, it purifies the logical defects inherent in the human knowledge system at the source. This thoroughly resolves the problems of high data impurity, low logical precision, and susceptibility to human bias contamination in native LLMs. Ultimately, through sustained drilling, reviewing, and correcting, the Transformer model autonomously acquires symbolic-logic-like higher-order logical capabilities that surpass ordinary humans and rival top scientists. This iterative process is irreversible, exhibits sustained compound-interest effects, and represents the only core pathway for LLMs to break through their current capability plateau.
